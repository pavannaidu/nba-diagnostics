# Canine Endocrine And Metabolic Diagnostic Guidance

This document supports a synthetic diagnostic recommendation workspace for dogs.

## Common intake patterns
- Increased thirst combined with appetite or weight change supports endocrine or metabolic screening.
- Chronic endocrine history should raise the priority of monitoring-oriented diagnostics.

## Common next diagnostic actions
- `FRUCTOSAMINE` is appropriate when diabetic screening or glycemic follow-up is the central question.
- `THYROID_PANEL` is appropriate when weight, appetite, lethargy, or thyroid follow-up is the question.
- `CORTISOL_SCREEN` is appropriate when endocrine concern includes cortisol-related screening or follow-up.
- `SDMA_CHEM17` can still be relevant when increased thirst widens the question to renal or metabolic status.

## Relative preference notes
- Prefer `FRUCTOSAMINE` when increased thirst and increased hunger suggest diabetic screening.
- Prefer `THYROID_PANEL` when lethargy and weight change dominate more than thirst.
- Prefer `CORTISOL_SCREEN` when the clinical question is broader endocrine screening rather than thyroid- or glucose-specific.

## Repeat-test cautions
- Avoid recommending the same endocrine monitoring test again inside the recent-test window unless the current intake clearly represents a new escalation.
- A recently completed endocrine test should usually move to a suppressed or why-not-selected explanation.
