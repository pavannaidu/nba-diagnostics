# Canine GI Diagnostic Guidance

This document supports a synthetic diagnostic recommendation workspace for dogs.

## Common intake patterns
- Vomiting, diarrhea, or recent diet change usually indicate a GI-oriented workup.
- Recurrent GI complaints with prior similar episodes should favor tests that can clarify chronicity or infectious risk.

## Common next diagnostic actions
- `GI_PANEL` is appropriate when the question is broad GI characterization, chronicity, or combined blood-based workup.
- `FECAL_PCR` is appropriate when diarrhea or suspected infectious GI disease is prominent.
- `FECAL_SCREEN` is more appropriate for preventive or lower-acuity stool evaluation than for complex active GI presentations.
- `WELLNESS_PANEL` may still be relevant when lethargy or broad systemic concern coexists with GI signs.

## Relative preference notes
- Prefer `GI_PANEL` over stool-only testing when the case needs broader GI characterization.
- Prefer `FECAL_PCR` when diarrhea is the clearest high-signal symptom.
- Treat preventive fecal screening as lower priority than active GI workup in symptomatic visits.

## Repeat-test cautions
- Do not repeat a recent GI diagnostic unless there is a clear worsening pattern or a new question.
- If a recent identical test exists, document the suppression as deliberate duplicate avoidance.
